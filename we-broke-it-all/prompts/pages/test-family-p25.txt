Black and white line art for a children's coloring book page, ages 4-7.

SCENE: A simple bedroom. Daughter 3 lies in bed under a plain blanket, head on a pillow, arms out on top of the blanket as single lines. Father sits upright on a plain chair beside the bed, one straight single-line arm resting on the edge of the blanket; a cup stands on a small bedside table. Through the window: a garden with a broken fence. No wilting or dying plants.

STYLE — follow exactly:
- Black and white line art for a children's coloring book page, ages 4 to 7.
- Deep Roots Kids flat-vector style for the WHOLE page: figures, animals, plants, buildings
  and objects all built from simple geometric shapes at one matching level of detail.
- Pure black outlines on a pure white background. No grey.
- Thick, bold, confident, uniform line weight suitable for crayons.
- Every shape a fully CLOSED region so colour cannot leak out.
- Large simple shapes, few objects, generous open space inside each shape.
- Flat front-on storybook composition, centered.
- Calm, gentle, honest mood. Sad moments are shown by how people stand and turn, never by darkness.

DO NOT INCLUDE:
Style:
- NO shading, hatching, crosshatching, stippling, gradients, solid black fills, texture.
- NO dark sky, NO night, NO large black or shadowed areas, NO long cast shadows.
- NO text, letters, numbers, words, names, labels, captions, signature, or border frame.
- NO naturalistic or realistic rendering of anything — no fur texture, no bark texture,
  no scales drawn one by one, no feathers drawn stroke by stroke.
- NO objects carried over from the style reference: no vegetables, pumpkins, cabbages,
  carrots, watering can, garden tools or dog UNLESS the SCENE itself names them.
- NO blank faces: every family-cast person seen from the front has eyes and a smile line.
Theological — theology-guardrails §0-§3, non-negotiable, WLC 109:
- NO depiction of God in any form. No face in the sky. No hands reaching from clouds or trees.
  No footprints. No rays of light arranged into a figure. No dove. No radiant humanoid shape.
  No eye. No throne. No giant figure over the world. No voice lines or sound marks from the sky.
- NO figure of Christ, and NO symbol standing for Him: no cross, no crown, no empty tomb,
  no shepherd's staff, no nail-marked hands, no halo.
- Where God acts in the text, depict only the RESULT of the act, never an actor.
- NO angels, NO wings on any person, NO winged or humanoid figure at any gate.
- NO light, glow, sparkle or rays coming from any person, tree, fruit or object.
Scope — Book Three (Gate 1 rulings):
- NOTHING from Genesis 3:15: no heel, no foot near the serpent, no crushed head, no woman with
  a child, no baby, no seed, no light on the horizon, no path leading anywhere (§2, F11).
- The serpent is a PLAIN SNAKE (D1): smooth simple body, simple head seen in profile, one plain
  round dot eye. NO fangs, NO forked tongue, NO horns, NO spikes, NO slit pupils, NO eyebrows,
  NO eyelashes, NO smile, NO big cartoon eyes, NO human features of any kind. Not cute, not scary.
- Fruit (D2): every tree in a scene bears the SAME small plain round fruit. The tree in the
  middle is not special: not larger, not glowing, not marked, no different fruit.
- NO fig leaves worn and NO leaves held (D3).
- NO sweat drops, NO dust clouds (D7). NO pregnancy, nothing of childbirth.
- NO death imagery anywhere: no grave, no bones, no dead or hurt animal, no blood, no wilting
  or dying plants, no fallen leaves (D9 — death is in the text only).
- NO animal near the garments, NO hide being cut or sewn, NO needle, NO tool (D4).
- NO mirror, NO reflection of a person in water or glass.

HUMAN FIGURES — follow exactly:
(reference B); CLOTHES come from the OUTFITS list, not from the sheet:
- Father: short rounded haircut. Mother: head wrap with wavy hair visible beneath it.
- Daughter 1: rounded locs and round glasses. Daughter 2: neat braids and round glasses.
- Daughter 3: braids with round beads. Son: wavy-curly hair.
- Dog: small, floppy ears. Cat: plain and simple.
- Draw ONLY the family members the SCENE names.
- Every family member has a simple face: two dot eyes and a small mouth line.
- STICK-FIGURE CONSTRUCTION, exactly as the figures in the style reference (reference A):
  every arm and leg is ONE thin single line, the same weight as the outline. A bent arm is one
  line that bends at the elbow. NEVER a fleshy, rounded, two-sided or shaped arm or leg.
- Hands are small mittens with ONE small thumb bump, exactly as on the cast sheet — NO
  separate fingers, NO knuckle or nail lines; one pointing gesture only where the SCENE names it.
  Feet are plain closed ovals, no shoes.
- Arms held away from the body with white space between arm and torso.
- Short sleeves end at the upper arm; the arm below is one single thin line. No long sleeves, no coats.
- No hats, hoods or caps — hair, glasses, beads and the head wrap always visible.
- Mid-shot of people DOING something, turned toward what they are doing. Never a close-up portrait.

OUTFITS — each person wears exactly this, and nothing else:
- Daughter 3: sleeveless nightgown
- Father: sleeveless round-neck top and knee-length shorts

FRAMING:
- All meaningful content within the inner 85% of frame.
- No element touching or crossing the frame edge.
- No horizontal line running unbroken edge to edge.
