CONCEPT A — "Not the end" (Genesis 3:23-24, as the book's last page):
In the MIDDLE band, the first man and woman seen FROM BEHIND, faces never visible, walking side by side away from the viewer across open grassland, HANDS APART. The man: short cropped hair, plain sleeveless one-shoulder tunic with an uneven hem. The woman: long wavy hair, plain sleeveless knee-length dress with an uneven hem. Behind and beyond them, smaller, the edge of a garden: a line of rounded leafy trees with an opening, and in the opening a single small upright sword outline with a few short straight light-lines close around it — no figure, no wings, no fire. Calm daylight, no sun on the horizon.
