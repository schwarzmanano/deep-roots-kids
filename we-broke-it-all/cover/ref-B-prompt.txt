BLACK AND WHITE LINE REFERENCE for a children's book FRONT COVER composition, portrait 3:4. Pure black outlines on white, no shading, no colour. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, letters, numbers or logos.

FIGURE CONSTRUCTION FIRST: every person is built like the reference sheet — round head, one simple flat clothing shape, and EVERY arm and EVERY leg a single thin line ending in a small oval hand or oval foot. No fleshy, shaped or two-sided limbs.

LAYOUT: TOP 40% open sky with two or three small clouds and nothing else (the title sits there). MIDDLE 45%: the scene. BOTTOM 15%: plain open ground with nothing on it (subtitle and series badge sit there).

NEVER: any depiction of God, any face or hands in the sky, light shaped into a figure, a sunrise or glowing light on the horizon, a path leading anywhere, a seed, a cross, angels or wings, any figure of Christ, apples, hand-holding.
