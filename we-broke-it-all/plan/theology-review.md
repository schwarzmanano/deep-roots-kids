## Decision points & Rulings

### D1 — The serpent (3:1 "more subtle than any animal of the field"; p5, p7, p17, p21)
**Ruling:** Option A (Plain snake, posture carries "subtle"). Eyes shown plain. Keep on pages p5, p7, p17, and p21. Do not name Satan in teaching.

### D2 — The fruit (3:6; the tree "in the middle of the garden"; p3, p7, p9)
**Ruling:** Option D. *Gate 2 Ruling (2026-10-02):* The forbidden tree bears plain round fruit (simple circles, no stem/leaf) only; all other trees are leafy with no fruit. 

### D3 — Clothing continuity (3:7 fig leaves; 3:21 garments of skin)
**Ruling:** Option C (Shame shown, leaves not worn). 

### D4 — 3:21: God clothing them (p29)
**Ruling:** Option B (The garments laid out as a gift). *Gate 2 Ruling (2026-10-02):* For worn garments of skin, use Option A vocabulary (plain sleeveless one-shoulder tunic and knee-length dress with uneven hems only, single thin line limbs).

### D5 — Shame and hiding (3:7-10; p11, p13, and p15 if D6 approves it)
**Ruling:** Option A (Body language only).

### D6 — Blame as a family-cast application (p15, p19)
**Ruling:** Option B (One family page — blame only). Page 15 is a first-couple page, Page 19 is the family application. Use Son (4) blaming the dog. *Gate 2 Ruling (2026-10-02):* Son's face is worried. The broken object is a clay cup with a clear crack. No confession or forgiveness scene is depicted before Book Seven, and p19's picture stays unresolved. Teaching lines may point the reader to God's forgiveness.

### D7 — The curse (3:16-19; p23)
**Ruling:** Thorns and thistles shown on p23. Omit sweat. Omit dust. Omit 3:16a. The woman is shown working alongside the man.

### D8 — Cherubim and the flaming sword (3:24; p31)
**Ruling:** Option D (Distance). *Gate 2 Ruling (2026-10-02):* Accept the larger figures for coloring clarity. The sword of light is drawn using short light-lines (gate-light attempt 1).

### D9 — Death: "to dust you shall return" (3:19)
**Ruling:** Option A (Text only, p23).

### D10 — Hope without stealing Book Four
**Ruling:** Option A (Hope from inside Genesis 3).
