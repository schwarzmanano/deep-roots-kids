Deep Roots Kids — Brand Color & Font Spec
Sep 17, 2026 · @Schwarz
Overview
This spec governs the visual identity of Deep Roots Kids — the children's discipleship arm of Deep Roots Ministry — across every printed and animated touchpoint: the Creation to Glorification teaching-video album, coloring books, worksheets, cards, and curriculum covers produced under DR-OS.
Deep Roots Kids is a sibling brand to the adult Deep Roots line, not a separate identity. The adult brand speaks in Scripture Gold, Deep Parchment, and Midnight Navy — a palette built for chiaroscuro lighting, cinematic gravity, and a grown congregation wrestling with faith and technology. Deep Roots Kids inherits the same theological bones — Christ-centered, covenantal, Word-saturated — but translates them into a warmer, brighter, more tactile register fit for small hands and young hearts. A parent should be able to set a Deep Roots Kids book next to a Deep Roots Adult resource and recognize the family resemblance at once, while a child should feel the room has been made for them.
Two visual worlds sit under this one brand:
The Family-Cast World — the flat-vector, stick-figure family of six (Father, Mother, three daughters, son, and family dog) who host, narrate, and model faith at home. This is the "character" identity of Deep Roots Kids.
The Golpo Painterly World — the warmer, textured backgrounds used in the teaching videos, built for movement and atmosphere behind the family cast.
This document specifies the color palette, typography, and illustration rules that keep both worlds — and every printed product built from them — visually unified.
Primary Palette
The core palette is built around the warm cream/parchment paper texture already established as the character bible's background, paired with a small set of anchor colors drawn from the same family as the adult brand's Scripture Gold and Deep Parchment — but lifted in value and warmth so the palette reads as inviting rather than reverent-and-weighty.

Fixed vs. flexible: hairstyle, glasses, beads, head wrap, and build are locked identity markers and never change. Clothing color and pattern may shift per scene, but should always be pulled from the Primary Palette so a character never wears a color outside the family system.
Creature/prop rule: animals, plants, and objects placed near a character are sized to her and simplified in detail when cropped close, and are colored from the same Primary Palette rather than introducing new hues.
Coloring Book Rules
Coloring pages are line art only — the Primary and Accent palettes above exist as a reference key for a child filling the page, not as printed color on the page itself.
Line weight: a single, clean, consistent black outline (#000000), matching the flat-vector family-cast style used elsewhere in Deep Roots Kids — no painterly, textured, or gradient linework, since painterly art has no clean outline to strip color from.
Style source: coloring pages use the flat-vector family-cast style only. The Golpo painterly background style is reserved for full-color products and is never converted into a coloring page.
What stays uncolored (white): the page background is left white for printing economy and contrast, even though Parchment Cream is the brand background everywhere else — the cream tone returns on the book's cover, section dividers, and any full-color pages.
What can carry pre-printed color: covers, chapter dividers, and an answer-key/reference thumbnail (showing the "correct" coloring using the Primary Palette) may be full color; interior activity pages stay black-and-white line art.
Genesis 1 exception: Book One (Creation to Glorification, Genesis 1) keeps its own naturalistic linework for creation imagery, but any human figures appearing in it (e.g., the image-of-God page) switch to the flat-vector character-bible style, so the book still visually bridges to the rest of the catalog. The naturalistic linework itself is held to the same black outline weight and Primary Palette as the family-cast style, so the bridge holds even where the rendering detail is higher.
Scripture text on the page: where a verse (WEB translation) appears on a coloring page, set it in the body serif specified under Typography, in Storybook Navy, never inside the coloring area itself.

Print rule: all three faces are free/open-license Google Fonts, so they embed cleanly in print-ready PDFs (coloring books, worksheets) without licensing friction, and match exactly what renders on the web/video side.
Illustration Style Rules
Three styles coexist under Deep Roots Kids. Each has a job; the rule below governs when each is used, and how they combine.
Flat-Vector Family-Cast Style — 2D flat stick-figure characters, clean black outline, minimal geometric shapes, flat solid color fills (from the Character & Scene Accent Palette), set against the Parchment Cream paper-textured background. This is the "face" of Deep Roots Kids — used for the family cast wherever they appear.
Golpo Painterly Background Style — the warmer, textured background style used behind the family cast in the Creation to Glorification teaching videos. Has no clean outline, so it cannot be stripped down into coloring-page line art.
Naturalistic Style — reserved for Book One (Genesis 1), used for creation imagery that isn't the family cast. To keep the book from reading like two different books stapled together, naturalistic elements are colored strictly from the Primary Palette and share the same clean black outline as the family-cast style, so the two visual worlds stay tied together even as rendering detail shifts.
The Hybrid Rule (full-color products): flat-vector character over a painterly background — the family cast keeps its clean outline and flat fills, composited onto the richer Golpo-style background. Creatures or objects placed near a character are sized to her and simplified in detail if cropped close, so they read as belonging to her world rather than competing with it.
The Coloring Rule: coloring pages use the flat-vector family style only. Painterly art is excluded by definition, since it has no outline to strip color from (see Coloring Book Rules above).
Governing question for any new asset: "Is this a full-color product (video still, album art, worksheet header, card) or a black-and-white activity page?" Full color → Hybrid Rule. Black-and-white → flat-vector line art only.

Governance & Asset Notes
This spec belongs in the DR-OS Knowledge Brain under Brand-System, alongside the Character-Bible folder, so any agent or collaborator building Deep Roots Kids assets pulls color and type from one place.
The "Deep Roots" tree-and-roots logo remains a fixed PNG asset, never redrawn — the same governance already locked for the adult brand's Title Card/End Card system applies here.
New accent colors or fonts are proposed against this spec before use, not introduced ad hoc inside a single book or episode.
As the Genesis 1 coloring book and future Creation to Glorification titles move through production, deviations worth keeping (like the Genesis 1 naturalistic exception, bridged by the shared outline weight and Primary Palette color rule) should be logged back into this document so the spec stays the single source of truth.
Source file standards: flat-vector cast assets are maintained as live .AI or .SVG files with unexpanded strokes, so outlines and fills stay editable; Golpo painterly backgrounds are maintained as layered .PSD files; print-ready coloring pages and worksheets are delivered as print-resolution PDF, with a flattened PNG/JPG export for web and video use.
