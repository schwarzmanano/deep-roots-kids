# Deep Roots Kids — Theology Guardrails

> GOVERNANCE TIER: standing series asset, same standing as
> [character-bible.md](character-bible.md) and [brand-spec.md](brand-spec.md).
> CONFESSIONAL AND PERMANENT. Governs every book in the Creation to Glorification series,
> and every future Deep Roots Kids product, without being re-stated per book.
>
> Every book's `plan/theology-review.md` must include a "Guardrails — verified against this
> plan" checklist covering each numbered rule below. A book-level ruling may narrow these
> rules; it may never relax them. Amending this file requires an explicit, dated ruling by
> the editor recorded in §Amendments.

## §0 — The governing principle (carried forward from genesis-1 DECISION-1)

No depiction of God in any form — Father, Son, or Holy Spirit. Where God acts in the text,
depict only the **result** of the act, never an actor performing it. No face in the sky, no
hands from clouds, no light arranged into a figure, no dove standing in for the Spirit, no
radiant humanoid shape, no eye.

**Confessional ground:** Westminster Larger Catechism Q.109 forbids "the making any
representation of God, of all or of any of the three persons, either inwardly in our mind,
or outwardly in any kind of image or likeness of any creature whatsoever."

## §1 — No human figure of Christ, ever (ruled 2026-09-27)

**No human figure of Christ will ever be depicted in this series — in any book, under any
circumstance.** WLC 109's "any of the three persons" includes the Son. The incarnation does
not lift the prohibition: an image of Jesus' humanity presents itself as an image of the
person, and the person is God.

This applies to every form of human figure: full figure, silhouette, figure from behind, at
distance, in shadow, in a crowd, as a baby in a manger, on a cross, as a reflection, or as
one flat-vector figure among others. There is no framing, scale or style that makes it
permissible.

**From Book Six onward**, Christ's presence and work are shown **symbolically only**. The
permitted vocabulary:

- the empty tomb
- the cross (empty — no body on it)
- light (never shaped into a figure — §0)
- a throne (empty, or with light only — no occupant)
- a crown
- nail-marked hands **with no face, head, or body attached**
- a shepherd's staff

Anything outside this list is proposed as a book-level decision before use, not introduced
ad hoc. Nail-marked hands are the edge case of the list: they must read as a sign of the
wounds, never as the extremities of a drawn body — no wrists continuing into arms, no
sleeves, no figure implied behind them.

## §2 — Before Book Six: promise shown as symbol, not person

Books before Six must not anticipate §1 by depicting Christ early. Where the text promises
Him — e.g. Book Four, "He Didn't Walk Away," Gen 3:15 — **the promised Rescuer is shown
symbolically only: a light, a path, a seed.** Never a person, never a baby, never a figure
on the horizon.

Where Books One–Five point to Christ in **teaching lines or back matter**, that is text
only; it carries no image obligation.

## §3 — Human figures never imply God has bodily form (genesis-1 DECISION-5, extended)

Human figures follow the flat-vector character-bible construction only. No composition may
imply God has a body: no human figure placed as God's "likeness" in a way that reads as a
portrait of God (mirror images captioned "like Him," a figure in the sky, a giant figure
over the world). The image of God is shown in what people **do** — love, know, choose the
good, work, care, worship — not in what they look like.

## §4 — Existing Hard NEVERs (series-wide, from genesis-1 Gate 1)

- No depiction of the face of the Father or of Christ (now subsumed and widened by §0–§1)
- No hands, dove, or light-shaped-as-figure standing in for God
- No strike-through or negation of a true statement
- No fabricated historical or scientific evidence

## §5 — People outside the family cast (ruled 2026-09-27, made-like-him D6)

People outside the family cast **may appear anywhere in the series where the text calls for
depicting humanity broadly** — crowds, neighbours, nations, "every person." They use the
flat-vector character-bible **construction technique** (circle head, single-line limbs, oval
hands and feet, sleeveless/at-or-above-knee garments, arms away from the torso) for
stylistic consistency, but carry **none** of the family cast's fixed identity markers: no
glasses with locs, no glasses with neat braids, no beaded braids, no head wrap over blonde
hair, no "footballer" cut on an East African father figure paired with the cast's build.
No outside figure may read as a stray family member. §1 still applies without exception: no
outside figure in any crowd, at any scale, represents Christ.

Diversity of humanity is shown **visually** — age, height, ability, hair, dress — not by
printed colour words applied to peoples.

## §6 — The divine name in WEB quotations (ruled 2026-09-27, made-like-him D8)

The series prints the World English Bible (genesis-1 DECISION-4). Where WEB renders the
divine name **"Yahweh"**, quotations **keep "Yahweh" exactly as WEB has it** — never
substitute "the LORD," and never switch to a WEB variant edition to avoid it.

At the name's **first appearance in each book**, that page's teaching line must first
explain the name. Requirement (a pointer, not wording): *Yahweh is God's own covenant name,
given to His people.* The author writes the line in each book (authorship-policy §8).

(Each book is read on its own by a family, so the line is due once per book, not once per
series.) _Amended 2026-09-29: this rule previously fixed an AI-originated sentence to be
printed verbatim; superseded by authorship-policy §6/§8._ Book-authored paraphrase may say "God"; the rule governs quotation. Scripture
quoted in the album's teaching script (ESV) is re-quoted in WEB on every printed page.

## §7 — Authorship (ruled 2026-09-29)

All reader-facing prose in every book is governed by
[authorship-policy.md](authorship-policy.md): AI never authors, rewrites or polishes prose
for publication; every text unit is registered in the book's `provenance.yaml`; a release
build refuses any unit that is AI-drafted, draft, unregistered or unresolved.
