## Rulings

### DECISION-1 — p3, the Spirit over the waters — DEPICT NOTHING
No dove, no face, no hand, no humanoid light. Confessional grounds: WLC 109
forbids representation of any person of the Trinity. Show the waters and the
darkness beginning to yield; let the absence itself carry the meaning.

### DECISION-2 — p13, lights vs. sun and moon — PLAIN NOUNS + TEACHING LINE
Use "sun" and "moon" on the page, for the child's vocabulary. Add a short
teaching line beneath preserving Genesis 1's polemic against sun and moon
worship. Plain word for the eye, the point for the parent to read aloud.

### DECISION-3 — p21, dinosaurs — EXCLUDE
This book takes no position on an origins question that divides faithful
readers in good conscience. Common land animals only, per Gen 1:24-25.

### DECISION-4 — translation — WEB
Public domain, ASV lineage, no licensing restriction on quotation.

### DECISION-5 — p25, the image of God — FINALIZED
Figures shown from behind, at distance, faces unseen, engaged in dominion-work
(tending, naming, exercising the charge just given in Gen 1:28) — not posed or
static. The image of God is shown in what they do, not in what they look like.
No clothing question to resolve, because no bodily likeness is being depicted at
all — this sidesteps Gen 2:25 entirely since there is no fall-state to get wrong.
No visual proximity to any depiction of God, consistent with ruling 1.

### DECISION-6 — p31, "day" — LEAVE PLAIN
Leave the word as Moses left it. No gloss, no position taken between 24-hour and
framework readings. That conviction is formed in the home, not on this page.
