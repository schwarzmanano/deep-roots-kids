## Rulings

### DECISION-1 — p5, Gen 2:7 — THE MOMENT AFTER
Show the moment after the forming, not the forming. One figure from behind at the garden's
edge at first light. No hand, no form, no breath or wind shape, nothing above him implying a
divine actor — consistent with genesis-1 DECISION-1 and guardrails §0.

### DECISION-2 — p5–p11, the first man and woman — DECISION-5 CARRIED; CLOTHING DISCLOSED
1. **Treatment:** genesis-1 DECISION-5 unchanged — from behind, at distance, faces unseen, at
   work; male and female by build and hair only; no cast markers.
2. **Clothing:** simple garments approved as a print convention — and **disclosed in the
   book's back matter**, not only recorded here. p33 carries, verbatim:
   > "For modesty in this children's edition, Adam and Eve are shown in simple garments.
   > Scripture tells us they were unashamed and without clothing before sin entered the world
   > (Genesis 2:25) — the picture reflects a publishing choice for young readers, not a claim
   > about what the text says."
   Garments stay plain and un-tailored so they never read as Gen 3:21's garments of skin.
3. **Eve's creation:** result only. No deep sleep, no rib (§0).

### DECISION-3 — family cast faces — APPROVED AS PROPOSED
Family cast fully faced per the character bible, on two binding conditions: every page's text
names an action, never an appearance; no close-up portraits, no single face centred under
"like Him" — every family page is a mid-shot of people doing something together.

### DECISION-4 — mirror metaphor — SPOKEN ONLY
Approved as proposed. The mirror is used verbally only (p31 teaching line). No mirror,
reflecting pool or glass in any scene. "Show His light" (p29) is a lamp on a stand, never
glow or rays from a person. "Reflect His face" is not printed.

### DECISION-5 — p27 — PRINTED POSITIVELY
Approved. p27 prints: "God made animals wonderful — but only people are made in His image."
No monkey or ape on the page. The audio keeps the teaching script as spoken.

### DECISION-6 — every person — SHOWN VISUALLY; NEW STANDING RULE
Approved. "Red, yellow, black or white" is not printed; diversity is shown visually — age,
height, ability, hair. **Standing series rule recorded as theology-guardrails.md §5:** people
outside the family cast may appear wherever the text calls for depicting humanity broadly,
built with the flat-vector construction technique, carrying none of the cast's fixed markers.

### DECISION-7 — p21 Psalm 139 — A BABY WELCOMED, UNNAMED
Approved, avoiding the seventh-child lock-in. A baby is welcomed and held — unnamed, with
their own (non-cast) parents, not established as belonging to the family. The same child ages
across the section: **infant on p21, toddler on p23, young child on p25.** Held consistent by
the child's own simple non-cast marker (two round hair puffs once hair shows) and the parents'
(mother: high round bun; father: short beard). Ruled 2026-09-27 via chat: across pages, since
the book is single-sided and has no facing spreads.

### DECISION-8 — translation and the divine name — KEEP "YAHWEH"; NEW STANDING RULE
Ruled distinctly from the recommendation: keep "Yahweh" as WEB has it; never substitute
"the LORD." **Standing series rule recorded as theology-guardrails.md §6.**
Applied here: p5 quotes Gen 2:7 in WEB verbatim — its first appearance in this book — and the
teaching line opens with "Yahweh is God's own special name — the name He gave His people to
call Him." p7 quotes Gen 1:27 in WEB. Both verified against ebible.org/eng-web 2026-09-27.
ESV quotations in the teaching script are re-quoted in WEB on the printed page.

### DECISION-9 — p3, "Let us make" — LEAVE PLAIN
Accepted as recommended. Text as Moses wrote it; no plural claim in the teaching line. The
fuller reading is formed in the home (the instinct of genesis-1 DECISION-6).

### DECISION-10 — p17, "choose" — FRAMED WITHIN GRACE
Accepted as recommended. Page text states the gift; the teaching line distinguishes Adam in
innocency (WCF 9.2) from us now (WCF 9.3 — "we need God's help… and He gives it") and ends
with Christ's obedience for us.

### DECISION-11 — p31, Christ the true Image — TEXT ONLY
Accepted as recommended. The family at evening worship around the open Bible; no cross, no
light-shape, no symbol for Christ (guardrails §1–§2). Text carries Col 1:15 and 3:10.

### DECISION-12 — scope boundary — EXCLUDED
Accepted as recommended. No tree of knowledge, no Gen 2:16-17 command (Book Three); generic
fruit trees only, no single central or marked tree. No angels drawn, angel lyric not printed.
